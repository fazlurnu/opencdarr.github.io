# L5 · Beyond two aircraft

!!! note "In preparation"
    The L5 notebooks are being written. The plan below is the commitment; the [curriculum document](https://github.com/fazlurnu/OpenCDaRR/blob/main/docs/curriculum.md) holds the live version.

Everything so far was pairwise. This level lifts that: encounters where the conflict is a crowd, not a pair, and where the resolver's answer to one intruder can create the next.

The planned lessons:

- **Rings** — N aircraft spawned on a circle, all converging on the centre at once; the stress test with a known worst case.
- **Traffic density** — random traffic over an area, and the density axis that safety curves are drawn against.
- **A mixed fleet** — multirotors and fixed-wings in the same airspace, and what a shared resolver does with asymmetric performance.

Read ahead: [Scenarios → Ring](../handbook/scenarios/ring.md) and [Scenarios → Random traffic](../handbook/scenarios/random-traffic.md), and the [random-traffic case study](../handbook/experiments/example-random-traffic.md).
