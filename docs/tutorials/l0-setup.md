# L0 · Setup

Three short lessons, about an hour and a half together. At the end you have an environment that runs the library and its tests, you have seen one complete answer — a probability with an interval — come out of a simulation you ran yourself, and you know where each public name lives. You will not understand the answer yet. That is deliberate: the rest of the course is the explanation, and it lands better once you know the shape of the thing being explained.
