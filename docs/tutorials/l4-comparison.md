# L4 · Comparing designs

!!! note "In preparation"
    The L4 notebooks are being written. The plan below is the commitment; the [curriculum document](https://github.com/fazlurnu/OpenCDaRR/blob/main/docs/curriculum.md) holds the live version.

A single rate answers "how safe is this stack". A comparison answers the question you actually have: "which of these is safer, and by how much, under the same conditions". This level teaches the experiment declaration — what stays `Fixed`, what gets a `Sweep` — and reads its output without fooling yourself.

The planned lessons:

- **The declaration** — `Fixed` and `Sweep`, and how the cross-product of sweeps becomes conditions.
- **What can vary** — every sweepable key, grouped by scenario, conflict, simulation, geometry, and component.
- **MVP vs VO** — two resolvers under one declaration, the canonical head-to-head.
- **The stack grid** — three axes swept at once, and how to read the grid that comes out.

Read ahead: [Experiments](../handbook/experiments/index.md) for the declaration, and the [pairwise case study](../handbook/experiments/example-pairwise-conflict.md) for what a finished comparison looks like.
