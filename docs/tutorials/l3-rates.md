# L3 · From runs to rates

!!! note "In preparation"
    The L3 notebooks are being written. The plan below is the commitment; the [curriculum document](https://github.com/fazlurnu/OpenCDaRR/blob/main/docs/curriculum.md) holds the live version.

One run is an anecdote. This level turns runs into a number: `estimate_p_los`, the sampled encounter behind it (`Draw` against a pinned value), and the reads that trip newcomers — `p_los_run` against `p_los_ac`, the measurement area, and why you add counts, never rates.

The planned lessons:

- **`estimate_p_los`** — one call, one probability with an interval.
- **The sampled encounter** — what `Draw` varies between runs, and what pinning a value does to the population you are measuring.
- **`p_los_run` vs `p_los_ac`** — per-run and per-aircraft are different denominators, and they answer different questions.
- **The measurement area** — where counting is allowed to happen.
- **Counts, not rates** — why batches combine by adding numerators and denominators, and what goes wrong when you average probabilities.

Read ahead: [Estimators](../handbook/estimators/index.md) and [Monte Carlo](../handbook/estimators/monte-carlo.md).
